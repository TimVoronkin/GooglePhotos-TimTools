Modules

Drag in Time

It allows you to select photos and, using an interactive window, drag and swap them in the timeline, and then confirm to save the desired time.

Smart Archiving

Keep your main timeline clutter-free by auto-archiving photos from selected albums (like a custom filters) with one-click toggles.

📱 Mobile Support (Bookmarklet)

Run Smart Archiving directly from your smartphone's browser (Android / iOS) without installing mobile extensions.

1. Drag the button below to your Bookmarks Bar (or click Copy Code and create a bookmark on your phone).
2. Open photos.google.com in your mobile browser.
3. Type the bookmarklet name in the address bar and tap it to launch the filter manager.
Drag to Bookmarks Bar

KML Geotag Export

It goes through all the selected photos and extracts from it GPS coordinates and timestamps and then exports them into a KML file for Google Earth.

Date Search & Picker

Adds a calendar button to the search bar and recognizes typed dates (e.g., "15 May" or "15.05.2023") to instantly jump to that date in your library.

Up Navigation Button

Adds an "Up" button in the photo viewer to jump straight back to the album or timeline grid without losing your scroll position.

Delete Shortcut

Press the physical Delete key while viewing a photo to open delete menu and then press Enter to confirm.

Quick Remove from Album

Adds ✕ button next to each album in the photo info panel to detach the current photo from that album instantly.

Theme & Accent Colors

Set the theme to match your Appearance setting in Google Photos (photos.google.com/settings under Appearance). TimTools icons and dialogs will adapt accordingly.
